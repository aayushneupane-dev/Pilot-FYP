How to Maximize Daily Earnings with a FlexRide Rental

Published September 7, 2026 · 5 min read

Renting a vehicle to drive for ride-sharing or delivery services gives you freedom over your work hours. Balancing rental costs with daily earnings, however, takes smart planning.

1. Target peak demand hours
Ride-share and delivery platforms operate on dynamic demand. Morning commuter hours (7 AM–10 AM) and evening rush hours (5 PM–9 PM) can be useful periods to test for stronger order volume and surge pricing.

2. Choose the right vehicle for your work

  • Scooters (125cc): Useful for food delivery in congested city centers where fuel efficiency and maneuverability matter.
  • Sedans & SUVs: Suited to ride-sharing platforms such as Pathao and inDrive for passenger trips.
  • Vans: Practical for bulk parcel deliveries and e-commerce logistics.

3. Keep payment methods ready
FlexRide supports digital payments through eSewa and Khalti, helping drivers renew or extend rental periods without a lengthy in-person process.
